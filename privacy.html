Privacy policy

SuperHSK · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: SuperHSK has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress is stored only on your phone. Data leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs it (AI exercises, speech recognition or sending feedback), and only what that feature needs.

Who we are

SuperHSK is an app for learning Chinese made by an independent developer. For any question about this policy, write to danapps.dev@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

Your course progress, reviews, settings and statistics are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Data that leaves your phone

FeatureAI exercises
What is sentThe text of the exercise and your answer. No name, no account, no contacts.
To whomThe AI service that answers (Groq or Google Gemini), through our server or directly.
FeatureSpeaking exercises
What is sentYour voice, while the microphone is on, to turn it into text.
To whomYour phone's speech recognition service (usually Google). The app does not keep recordings.
FeatureOpinions and “report a problem”
What is sentWhat you write, the exercise you were on, the app version, the phone model and the app language.
To whomOur feedback server, which forwards it to the developer.
FeatureCrash reports
What is sentThe technical error, the app version, the phone model and the app language. No personal content.
To whomOur feedback server, so we can fix the bug.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not use it to identify you.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only for speaking exercises, and only when you start one.
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder, if you turn it on.
  • Internet: for AI exercises, speech recognition and feedback. The rest of the app works offline.

Children

SuperHSK is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings to delete it. To ask us to delete a feedback message you sent, write to danapps.dev@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
