Privacy policy

SuperHSK – Learn Chinese · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: SuperHSK has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress is stored only on your phone. Data leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs it (AI exercises, speech recognition or sending feedback), and only what that feature needs.

Who we are

SuperHSK is an app for learning Chinese made by an independent developer. For any question about this policy, write to CONTACT_EMAIL.

Data stored on your phone

Your course progress, reviews, settings and statistics are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Data that leaves your phone

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
AI exercises (conversations, corrections)The text of the exercise and your answer. No name, no account, no contacts.The AI service that answers (Groq or Google Gemini), through our server or directly.
Speaking exercisesYour voice, while you hold the microphone, to turn it into text.The speech recognition service of your phone (usually Google). The app does not keep recordings.
Opinions and “report a problem”What you write, the exercise you were on, the app version, the phone model and the app language.Our feedback server, which forwards it to the developer.
Crash reportsThe technical error, the app version, the phone model and the app language. No personal content.Our feedback server, so we can fix the bug.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising, and we do not use it to identify you.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only for speaking exercises, and only when you start one.
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder, if you turn it on.
  • Internet: for AI exercises, speech recognition and feedback. The rest of the app works offline.

Children

SuperHSK is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Everything stored by the app is on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings to delete it. To ask us to delete a feedback message you sent, write to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
